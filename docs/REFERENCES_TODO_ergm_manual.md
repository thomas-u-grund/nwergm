# References of the nwergm manual: verification (2026-10-25)

All 18 entries of the bibliography of manual/nwergm_manual.tex were checked against Crossref (authors, year, title, journal, volume, issue, pages, DOI) or, for the books and the journal without a DOI, against library catalog records and the author's copy of the paper.

Corrected: Geyer and Thompson (1992), pages 657--683 (the manual said 699). Added: DOIs of the 15 articles; pages 1--40 of Snijders (2002); the subtitle of Kapferer (1972).

Confirmed without change: Hunter et al. 2008 (JSS 24(3)), Handcock et al. 2008 (JSS 24(1)), Hunter and Handcock 2006, Hunter, Goodreau and Handcock 2008, Handcock and Gile 2010, Krivitsky and Handcock (JRSSB 76(1), 29--46; online 2013, issue of January 2014), Robins et al. 2007, Wasserman and Pattison 1996, Frank and Strauss 1986, Hummel et al. 2012, Hunter 2007, Morris et al. 2008, Krivitsky 2012, Padgett and Ansell 1993.

Without DOI, confirmed from catalog records: Lazega 2001 (Oxford University Press; ISBN 0199242720), Kapferer 1972 (Manchester University Press; ISBN 0719004349), Snijders 2002 (Journal of Social Structure 3(2); pages from a bibliographic database and the author's draft, not from the journal site, which could not be reached).

Open: the website pages nwergm/citation.md quote page ranges 1-29 (JSS 24(3)) and 1-11 (JSS 24(1)); the jstatsoft.org pages could not be reached from here, Crossref has no page data for these two records. The manual gives no page range for them.
